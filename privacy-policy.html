Privacy Policy - Mextros

Last updated: May 2026

Mextros ("we", "our", or "us") operates a peer-to-peer metro parcel delivery platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number — required for account creation and OTP verification
  • Name and username — for profile identification
  • Email address — optional, for account recovery
  • Profile photo — optional, uploaded by user
  • KYC documents — Aadhaar and PAN card photos for identity verification
  • Location data — collected during active deliveries to track metro station
  • Device information — FCM token for push notifications
  • Chat messages — between sender and traveler for delivery coordination
  • Wallet and transaction data — for payment processing

2. Location Data Usage

Mextros collects foreground location data for the following purposes:

  • To detect the current metro station of the traveler during an active delivery
  • To show real-time delivery progress to the sender and receiver
  • Location is only accessed when the user is actively performing a delivery as a traveler and the app is open
  • Location data is not collected when no delivery is active
  • Location data is never sold to third parties

Users can disable location access at any time through device settings. Disabling location will prevent real-time station tracking during deliveries.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve our delivery services
  • To verify identity via OTP and KYC
  • To track delivery status in real-time
  • To process payments via Razorpay
  • To send delivery notifications via FCM
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

4. Data Sharing

  • Sender's phone number is shared with the assigned traveler
  • Receiver's phone number is shared with the assigned traveler
  • Payment data is processed by Razorpay (PCI DSS compliant)
  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party

5. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. KYC documents are stored securely with restricted access. Wallet transactions are processed through secure escrow mechanisms.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Transaction records are kept for 7 years for legal compliance. You may request account deletion via Settings in the app.

7. User Rights

  • Access your personal data through the app
  • Delete your account via Settings → Delete Account
  • Disable location access through device settings
  • Request data export by contacting support
